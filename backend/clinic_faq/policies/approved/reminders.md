# Synthetic Example — Appointment Reminders

**Fictional example only. This is not a real clinic policy.**

In this fictional example, an appointment reminder may be sent using the
contact method selected for a fictional booking. A reminder is a courtesy and
does not replace checking the appointment confirmation.

If a reminder is not received, the person may check the fictional online
booking page or contact the fictional scheduling desk to confirm the
appointment details.

This example does not promise a reminder delivery time or support a real
clinic's communication process.
